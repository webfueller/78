Three ways in, cheapest first. The first one takes about thirty seconds and asks for nothing.

  1. Try one thread. Paste a conversation — no account, and the copy it builds lives in memory for one request.
  2. Use your real mail. Gmail Takeout gives you an mbox; most calendars export ICS. Both stay on this machine. antechamber import --mbox mail.mbox --ics cal.ics --me you@example.com
  3. Look around first. A seeded synthetic life, so you can see the whole thing working before deciding whether to feed it anything real. antechamber demo
